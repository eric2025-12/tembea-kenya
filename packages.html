What's Included in Our Packages?

Each travel package is designed to help visitors enjoy different experiences across Kenya.

3-Day Safari

Destination: Maasai Mara

Coastal Escape

Destination: Diani Beach

Duration: 4 Days

Includes beach relaxation, coastal sightseeing and opportunities to experience local culture and water-based activities.

Adventure Package

Destination: Mount Kenya

Duration: 5 Days

Includes guided nature experiences, hiking opportunities and exploration of Kenya's mountain landscapes.
