Wildlife and Safari Highlights

The Maasai Mara offers visitors an opportunity to experience Kenya's remarkable wildlife and natural landscapes.

The Big Five

Visitors can look out for lions, elephants, leopards, buffaloes and rhinos during guided game drives.

The Great Migration

Between July and October, large herds of wildebeest and zebras move across the Mara ecosystem in search of fresh grazing.

Bird Watching

The reserve is home to many bird species, making it an interesting destination for bird lovers and nature enthusiasts.

Tips for Visitors

  • Carry comfortable clothing and suitable walking shoes.
  • Bring a camera and binoculars for wildlife viewing.
  • Follow the instructions of your safari guide.
  • Respect wildlife and avoid littering.
  • Book accommodation and safari activities in advance.
